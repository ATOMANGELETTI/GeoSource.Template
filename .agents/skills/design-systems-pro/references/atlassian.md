# Atlassian Design System Specification

Atlassian Design System powers Jira, Confluence, and Bitbucket. It features bold primary blue (`#0747A6`), structured issue cards, subtle rounded badge indicators, and high accessibility standards.

---

## 🎨 Color Tokens

- **Brand Bold**: `#0747A6` (Atlassian Blue)
- **Brand Hover**: `#0052CC`
- **Surface Default**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Sunken**: `#F4F5F7`
- **Text Default**: `#172B4D`
- **Text Subtler**: `#6B778C`
- **Border Default**: `#DFE1E6`

---

## 🔤 Typography (Charlie / Circular / Segoe UI)

- **Heading 800**: 29px / Bold (700)
- **Heading 500**: 20px / SemiBold (600)
- **Body Default**: 14px / Regular (400) / 20px line-height

---

## 📐 Shape & Focus

- **Border Radius**: `3px` (Buttons & Input Fields), `8px` (Cards & Dialogs)
- **Focus Ring**: `2px solid #4C9AFF`
