# IBM Carbon Design System Specification

IBM Carbon is a high-density, open-source design system for enterprise products and cloud software. It uses a strict 16-column layout, 2px border strokes, crisp contrast themes (White, g10, g90, g100), and bold blue accent scales.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Tokens (White / g10 Theme)

- **Interactive Primary**: `#0F62FE` (Carbon Blue 60)
- **Primary Hover**: `#0353E9`
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` (White) / `#F4F4F4` (Gray 10)
- **Layer 01**: `#F4F4F4`
- **Layer 02**: `#E0E0E0`
- **Text Primary**: `#161616` (Gray 100)
- **Text Secondary**: `#525252` (Gray 70)
- **Border Subtle**: `#E0E0E0`
- **Border Strong**: `#8D8D8D`

---

## 🔤 Typography (IBM Plex Sans)

- **Product Heading 05**: 28px / Regular (400)
- **Product Heading 03**: 20px / Regular (400)
- **Body Long 01**: 14px / Regular (400) / 20px line-height
- **Label 01**: 12px / Regular (400)

---

## 📐 Grid & Borders

- **Grid Baseline**: 16px mini-unit (4px sub-grid)
- **Border Radius**: `0px` (Strict sharp edges) or `2px` (Softened elements)
- **Focus Ring**: `2px solid #0F62FE` with `2px` offset
